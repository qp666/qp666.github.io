第一步:打开浏览器调试中的Network,找到要拷贝的请求,单击鼠标右键,然后一步步选择copy as cURL(复制)

![image.png](https://p1-juejin.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-k3u1fbpfcp/c4d4649ac3ef4d10b55dc471cf378fa3~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-in-crop-mark:1512:0:0:0.awebp)

第二步:打开postman中左上角的import(导入) 第三步:选择弹窗中的Raw text(原始文本)

![image.png](https://p3-juejin.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-k3u1fbpfcp/280c2cc5c5954487830a9c0f17d6f39e~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-in-crop-mark:1512:0:0:0.awebp)

第四步:把刚刚复制的粘贴进去 第五步:点击确定

![image.png](https://p6-juejin.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-k3u1fbpfcp/6374a61941834c29bf753058bdb142a5~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-in-crop-mark:1512:0:0:0.awebp) 第六步:继续点击确定

![image.png](https://p6-juejin.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-k3u1fbpfcp/54d8c1be74d54d1fb3381be57ba6c617~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-in-crop-mark:1512:0:0:0.awebp) 第七步:点击send发送请求

![image.png](https://p1-juejin.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-k3u1fbpfcp/1023a59d64404f098e0a256132a33731~tplv-k3u1fbpfcp-zoom-in-crop-mark:1512:0:0:0.awebp)
